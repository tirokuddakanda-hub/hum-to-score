Hum to Score

Hum a melody, and this turns it into sheet music you can play back, then export as MusicXML and MIDI for MuseScore, Dorico, Sibelius or a DAW.

1 · Your hum

  • Hum one melody on "da" or "doo", steady and not too quiet, in a quiet room.
  • Keep a steady beat. Counting "1, 2, 3, 4" out loud first is fine.
  • On iPhone, use Voice Memos: Settings → Voice Memos → Recording Mode: Stereo, then Audio Quality: Lossless.

2 · Score

Pitch you hummedNotes it heard

3 · Adjust

Tempo is guessed from your hum. If the notes look twice as long or short as they should, try ½× or 2×.

4 · Take it further

Open the .musicxml file in MuseScore (free) to edit notes, add harmony and parts. Drop the .mid file into GarageBand, Logic or any DAW to produce it as a track.

Everything runs in your browser. Your recording never leaves this page.
